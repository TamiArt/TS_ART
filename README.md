# TS·ART — TATIANA · ART · QI

**TS·ART** is the proposed personal art brand of Tatiana, bringing together traditional painting, drawing, digital art, creative education, and a philosophy of creative flow.

> **See. Feel. Create.**

This repository is the source of truth for the brand concept, online art academy, curriculum, product principles, and implementation roadmap. These documents describe the planned product; they do not imply that the academy has already been built.

## Brand architecture

- **TS·ART** — master personal art brand.
- **TATIANA · ART · QI** — brand signature / interpretation.
- **TS·ART Studio** — proposed name for the art studio and online academy.
- **QI Flow** — proposed name for creative practices and the learning philosophy.

QI is used as a poetic metaphor for creative energy, attention, movement, and flow—not as a scientifically established mechanism.

## Product vision

Build an accessible, structured online art academy for beginners through independent artists. Learners should be able to study at their own pace on a phone, tablet/iPad, or desktop, practise in short sessions, upload work, assess progress against clear criteria, and build a portfolio.

The academy should unite academic foundations with practical experimentation, traditional oil painting, digital art, art history, and the development of an individual visual language.

## Principles

1. Teach observable skills, not vague promises.
2. Distinguish verified historical/material facts from experiments and teaching hypotheses.
3. Explain why a technique works and when it may fail.
4. Support independent practice and reflection, not imitation alone.
5. Keep the experience accessible, modular, and usable across devices.
6. Do not promise professional mastery within a fixed number of months.
7. Check domain, social-handle, and trademark availability before treating the name as legally or commercially cleared.
