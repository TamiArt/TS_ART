# TS·ART — Brand Identity

## 1. Core identity

- **Primary brand:** TS·ART
- **Brand signature:** TATIANA · ART · QI
- **Core phrase:** See. Feel. Create.
- **Proposed studio / academy:** TS·ART Studio
- **Proposed creative-practice format:** QI Flow

TS·ART is intended to be a personal art brand that can grow beyond a single course or medium. It should represent the artist, artworks, educational products, experiments, and a future online academy under one coherent identity.

## 2. Name logic

- **TS** — the artist's initials / personal identity.
- **ART** — the central practice: painting, drawing, visual exploration, and digital art.
- **QI** — a symbolic reference to creative energy, attention, movement, and the state of flow.
- **Studio** — a place to learn, practise, experiment, and develop an individual artistic language.

**TATIANA · ART · QI** is a brand signature, not a claim that the letters have one objectively established etymology.

## 3. Brand architecture

### TS·ART — master brand
Used for the artist's identity, portfolio, paintings, digital artwork, author projects, and public presence.

### TS·ART Studio — education and studio
Used for the online academy, courses, structured learning paths, workshops, exercises, and learner portfolios.

### QI Flow — creative practice
A possible named format for short exercises, mindful observation, experimentation, and getting into a working rhythm. This is a proposed sub-brand and can be renamed after testing with the intended audience.

## 4. Positioning

A contemporary artist-led learning space that combines strong artistic foundations with experimentation and the freedom to develop a personal visual language.

**Audience:** adults starting art from zero, learners returning after a break, and traditional or digital artists who want stronger foundations. Design for people who may only have short periods available for practice.

**Promise:** a clear route from first observations and basic skills to independent projects and a documented portfolio.

Avoid guarantees such as “become a professional artist in 30 days.” Progress depends on practice, prior experience, feedback, and the complexity of the chosen medium.

## 5. Brand personality

- Thoughtful, artistic, and approachable.
- Contemporary but not trend-dependent.
- Calm and confident rather than loud or mystical.
- Technically curious, observant, and experimental.
- Encouraging without pretending that art has instant results.

## 6. Visual direction — initial hypothesis

The visual system is not final and should be explored through several logo concepts.

### Palette
- Warm ivory / paper.
- Sand, beige, and muted wood tones.
- Ink / deep charcoal for typography.
- A restrained accent colour derived from a brush mark or pigment.

### Typography
- A distinctive, highly legible wordmark.
- Editorial typography for longer learning content.
- Keep the dot in **TS·ART** as a deliberate signature element.
- Test legibility at small sizes: avatars, watermarks, artwork signatures, and mobile headers.

### Graphic language
Explore three directions:
1. **Typographic:** TS·ART alone, precise spacing, dot as the key detail.
2. **Artist's line:** one fluid brush-like line that suggests movement without looking generic.
3. **Flow symbol:** an abstract, restrained mark suggesting movement, attention, or a current.

Do not make the identity dependent on a literal yin-yang icon, stereotypical Eastern motifs, or an overly mystical aesthetic. QI should be felt through the design, not forced into a cliché.

### Image and photography
Prioritise genuine artwork, material details, pigment studies, sketchbook pages, brush marks, studies, and process. Keep mockups and AI-generated visuals clearly distinct from actual finished artworks.

## 7. Voice and copy

Use clear, thoughtful language. Explain techniques with concrete examples. Avoid empty motivational language and unsubstantiated claims.

Working slogan options:
- **See. Feel. Create.**
- **Find your creative flow.**
- **Learn the craft. Find your voice.**
- «Видеть. Чувствовать. Создавать.»
- «Осваивать мастерство. Находить собственный язык.»

These are options, not final approved campaign lines.

## 8. Naming and legal checks

Before public launch:
- Check exact and similar names in relevant trademark databases for intended markets and classes.
- Check domain availability and social handles.
- Check pronunciation, spelling, and search ambiguity in English and Russian.
- Test the dot and wordmark at small sizes.
- Obtain appropriate legal advice if a trademark application or commercial launch is planned.

No claim of trademark, domain, or social-handle availability has been made in this document.
