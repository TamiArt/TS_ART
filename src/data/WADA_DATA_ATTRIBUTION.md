# Wada Sanzo color data attribution

The Volume I dataset in `wadaSanzoData.json` is derived from [mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations](https://github.com/mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations), compiled by Matt DesLauriers from the original compilation by Dain M. Blodorn Kim.

The dataset is distributed under the MIT License. Copyright (c) 2020 Matt DesLauriers. The MIT license notice and permission terms apply to the dataset. The original color combinations are attributed to Sanzo Wada, *A Dictionary of Color Combinations*, Vol. 1.

The application includes 159 named colors and reconstructs combinations 1–348 by inverting the color-to-combination references in the dataset. HEX, RGB and LAB values are digital approximations of printed swatches and may differ from physical book colors.
